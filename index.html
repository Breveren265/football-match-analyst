How to use V2

  1. Enter the match and date.
  2. Record each team's last five results and goals scored/conceded in those five matches.
  3. Add head-to-head results only if they are recent and relevant.
  4. Include injuries, suspensions, travel and fixture congestion in the notes.
  5. Enter bookmaker 1-X-2 odds if you want to compare the market's implied percentages.
  6. Analyse the match, then save it to your list.
  7. Open Accumulator to select saved matches, enter the exact odds available on your 1Win account and calculate potential returns.

Understanding the output

Form points: win = 3, draw = 1, loss = 0. Comparison score: a weighted indicator based on recent form, goal difference and head-to-head. It is not a true win probability.

1 means home win; X means draw; 2 means away win. 1X means home win or draw; X2 means draw or away win.

Market implied percentage: approximately 100 ÷ decimal odds. The bookmaker margin means the three percentages usually add up to more than 100%.
